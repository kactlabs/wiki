# Throw the ball
## Objective:

- To encourage participation, interaction, and ensure everyone gets a chance to speak during the Zoom meeting in a fun and engaging way.

## How It Works:

- One person starts by saying the name of another participant and says,
“I’m throwing the ball to [Name]”
- The named person becomes the next speaker. Before throwing the ball, they must:
    - Answer the questions asked to them 
    - Give an intro, if asked 
- After speaking, they “throw the ball” to another participant by calling out,
“I’m throwing the ball to [Next Name]”.

## Rules:

- Everyone must participate when the ball is thrown to them.
- No one can throw the ball back to the person who just passed it.
- Be respectful and attentive while others are speaking.
- Make sure everyone gets a turn — no repeating names until all have spoken.
