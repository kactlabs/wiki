# e4e / Education 4 Everyone

## Laptop Eligibility Program

### Laptop Value & Distribution
- 10K ₹ worth of laptop eligible for students
- Release laptop every 3 months
- 4 laptops yearly
- Based on the student's learning, we will upgrade the laptop to MacBook after 12 months (MacBook is worth 30–60K ₹)

### Eligible Criteria
- Must have got at least 1st rank 20+ times from Grade 1 to Grade 8
- Must be 13+ years old
- Household income should be less than 8L/year

### Learning Requirements
- Students must attend at least 2 sessions per week during non-exam times
- Students must attend at least 1 session per month during exam times
- Students must be documenting all in pynotes, mylearning, tpython
