# 📌 Rules for "Python 4 EarlyZBirds" Program

We want only serious and consistent learners. Please follow these rules strictly.  
Violators will be removed immediately. 🚫

---

## 1. Attendance Discipline
- You must attend **at least 1 session per week**.  
- Missing **2 consecutive weeks** without notice = removal.  

## 2. Advance Intimation
- If you cannot attend the next day’s session, update in the group **before the session starts**.  
- Repeated last-minute excuses = violation.  

## 3. Active Participation
- Participate in coding challenges, quizzes, or discussions at least **once a week**.  
- Silent observers who never engage will be removed.  

## 4. Task Submission
- Weekly assignments must be submitted on time.  
- Consistently missing submissions = inconsistency = removal.  

## 5. Code of Conduct
- Respect mentors & peers.  
- No abuse, spam, or distractions — violation = removal.  

## 6. Penalty & Re-Entry
- Violators will be removed.  
- To rejoin, pay a **penalty fee (₹1K–₹3K)**.  
- Repeat offenders will **not be allowed back**.  

## 7. Scholarship Eligibility
- Only students with **80%+ attendance & 100% assignment submission** are eligible for  
  **up to 100% scholarship** on our GenAI Bootcamp (worth ₹45,000).  

## 8. Early Bird Punctuality
- Be on time ⏰ — joining **1+ min late** for 3 sessions in a row = warning.  
- Repeated delays = removal.  

## 9. Honesty Rule
- Fake attendance, copied assignments, or misuse of group resources = **permanent removal**.  

---

✅ Follow the rules. Stay consistent. Win the scholarship. 🚀
