## Intern Payment Structure

**Note:** Your stipend is based on your total contribution hours

| Hours               | Min     | Max     | Payment Mode |
| ------------------- | ------- | ------- | ------------ |
| 550 Hours           | ₹ 1000 | ₹ 5000 | One Time     |
| For every 250 Hours | ₹ 2000 | ₹ 7000 | Repeat       |

Note: Stipend will change based on the performance and regular attendance. However, Internship certificate will be provided for months they with us.


## Full Time Stipend Structure

**Contribution Hours:** 12 hrs/day, 6 days a week

| Duration | Monthly Stipend | Bond | Leaving Fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 Months training | ₹ 0 - 1K | - | - |
| 9-12 months employment | ₹ 5K - 8K/month | - | - |
| 13-16 months | ₹ 8K - 13K/month | 1 Year Bond (next 12 months) | 60% Stipend |
| 17-24 months | ₹ 13K - 25K/month | 1 Year Bond (next 8 months) | 60% Stipend |

**Note:** Stipend depends on performance. Internship certificate will be provided for months worked with us.
