## FOSS

FOSS (Free and Open Source Software) refers to software that is both free to use and has its source code openly available for anyone to inspect, modify, and distribute. It promotes collaboration, transparency, and community-driven development, allowing developers worldwide to contribute to projects and improve software collectively. Popular FOSS projects include Linux, Python, PostgreSQL, and Kubernetes, which power much of the modern internet and enterprise infrastructure.
