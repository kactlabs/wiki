# Intern & Employee Rulebook

## 1. Work Culture & Philosophy

We operate with a gamified military-style learning approach where punctuality and discipline are paramount. As a lean startup, we maintain structured schedules to maximize productivity and learning outcomes. Every minute counts in our fast-paced environment.

## 2. Attendance & Scheduling

### 2.1 Punctuality Standards
- **2.1.1** Punctuality is non-negotiable. Being even one minute late is considered a violation of our standards.
- **2.1.2** Slot changes require at least 2 hours advance notice with a valid reason.
- **2.1.3** Maximum of 3 slot changes permitted per week.

### 2.2 Work Schedule
- **2.2.1** Take 1.5 days off during weekdays to maintain work-life balance.
- **2.2.2** Weekend availability is expected for maximum productivity.
- **2.2.3** One weekend off per month is permitted with team coordination to avoid conflicts.

### 2.3 External Events
- **2.3.1** Attendance at external events (hackathons, conferences, etc.) is limited to once every 3 months.
- **2.3.2** Provide 4 days advance notice for any external event participation.

## 3. Daily Work Practices

### 3.1 Task Management
- **3.1.1** Create Trello tasks at the start of each work session with:
  - **3.1.1.1** Clear title and description
  - **3.1.1.2** Assigned team members
- **3.1.2** Update tasks upon completion with:
  - **3.1.2.1** CH (Contributed Hours)
  - **3.1.2.2** One-line summary of accomplishments

### 3.2 Break Protocol
- **3.2.1** Use "BRB" to indicate quick breaks
- **3.2.2** Specify duration: BRB5 (5 minutes), BRB15 (15 minutes), etc.

### 3.3 End-of-Day Requirements
Before logging off, complete the following:
- **3.3.1** Update Trello tasks with CH (Contributed Hours)
- **3.3.2** Post effort logs in Slack using @KBot

#### 3.3.3 Effort Log Format
```
@KBot
efl
Task 01: <one-line task description>
    - L1 - Success - 07:30 - 08:30
Task 02: <one-line task description>
    - L3 - Success - 08:30 - 11:00
Task 03: <one-line task description>
    - L1 - Success - 11:00 - 11:48
Total Working Hours: 4.18 hrs
```

**Example:**
```
@KBot
efl
Task 01: 10 snippets in tact-python, 3 snippets in pynotes
    - L1 - Success - 07:30 - 08:30
Task 02: Session About Python chapter 8
    - L3 - Success - 08:30 - 11:00
Task 03: Session with varun
    - L1 - Success - 11:00 - 11:48
    
Total Working Hours: 4.18 hrs
```

**Note:** Total Working Hours = Contributed Hours (total hours worked)

## 4. Weekly Requirements

### 4.1 Sunday Night (10:00 PM)
- **4.1.1** Submit your "Weekly Availability Schedule" for the upcoming week.

### 4.2 Saturday Deliverables
- **4.2.1** Complete 7 Substack articles per week
- **4.2.2** All articles must be published under articlevil.substack.com
- **4.2.3** If incomplete by Saturday, your manager will schedule a session to complete them together

### 4.3 Saturday Night Gaming (11:00 PM - 1:00 AM)
- **4.3.1** Non-tech gaming session with winner selection
- **4.3.2** Eligibility requirements:
  - **4.3.2.1** Minimum 3 working days in the week
  - **4.3.2.2** Minimum 20 contributed hours for the week

## 5. Performance Tracking

- **5.1** All effort logs are tracked via @KBot to maintain accurate records of contributions and progress.
- **5.2** This data helps us recognize top performers and identify areas for improvement.
