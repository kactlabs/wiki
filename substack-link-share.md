# How to Publish and Share Your Substack Link

This guide outlines the steps to publish an article on Substack and obtain a shareable link.

*  **Access Substack:** Log in or sign up to your Substack account.
![Step 1](substack_images/1.png)

*  **Go to Dashboard:** Click your profile icon and select "Dashboard."
![Step 2](substack_images/2.png)

*  **Start New Post:** From the left sidebar, click "Create new" and choose "Text post."
![Step 3](substack_images/3.png)

*  **Compose Article:** Write your article on the creation page.
![Step 4](substack_images/4.png)

*  **Insert Images:** Only use images from Unsplash or your own creations.
![Step 5](substack_images/5.png)

*  **Credit Unsplash Images:** When using Unsplash images, edit the caption to credit the creator.
![Step 6](substack_images/6.png)

*  **Proceed to Publish:** Click "Continue" at the top right.

*  **Set Publishing Options:** Define your audience, comment settings, add relevant tags, and select delivery preferences.
![Step 8](substack_images/8.png)

*  **Publish Article:** Click "Send to everyone now" at the bottom right.

* **Copy Shareable Link:** On the next page, find the "Share your link" section and click "Copy link."<br>
