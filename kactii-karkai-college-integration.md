## **KACTII ACADEMY**  
*Global Talent Pipeline Partnership for South Indian Engineering Institutions*

## EXECUTIVE PROPOSAL

Kactii Academy proposes a strategic partnership with premier South Indian engineering colleges to establish a **dual-stage international talent development program**. This initiative identifies, trains, and deployates first-year engineering students into paid internships with Canadian AI startups—creating a direct pathway from first-year coursework to global employment while elevating institutional placement excellence and international reputation.

---

## THE SECTOR CHALLENGE

South Indian engineering institutions operate in an intensely competitive landscape where differentiation requires more than academic rigor alone.

- **Curriculum Velocity Gap**: Traditional four-year curricula cannot keep pace with Generative AI advancements, leaving 78% of graduates requiring additional credentialing for industry readiness (*NASSCOM, 2024*)
- **Placement Metric Pressure**: International placement rates below 3% limit institutional ranking, student acquisition, and alumni prestige in the global market
- **Early Talent Flight**: High-achieving students seek overseas opportunities post-graduation, depriving domestic industry of innovation leadership and reducing alumni network value
- **Skills-Employability Mismatch**: Students gain theoretical proficiency but lack production-level software engineering experience demanded by global startups

Colleges that solve this alignment problem will dominate next-generation student recruitment.

## THE KACTII SOLUTION: A TWO-STAGE COMPETENCY ARCHITECTURE

We transform first-year students into internationally marketable GenAI engineers through a rigorous, milestone-gated progression.

### **Stage 1: Foundation Certification (550 Hours)**

- **Target**: First-year engineering students (CSE, IT, ECE, AI/ML specializations)
- **Curriculum**: 
  - Advanced Python & Data Structures
  - Software Engineering Principles & GitOps
  - Introduction to Machine Learning & Neural Networks
  - Database Design & Cloud Fundamentals
  - 3 CAPSTONE PROJECTS with code review metrics

- **Assessment Protocol**: 
  - 6 proctored technical examinations (pass threshold: 85%)
  - 2 live project demonstrations
  - Algorithmic complexity evaluation

- **Outcome**: Kactii Level-1 Certification (typically achieved by top 30% of cohort)

### **Stage 2: Production Engineer Specialization (550 Hours)**

- **Target**: Level-1 certified students only
- **Curriculum**:
  - Large Language Model Architecture & Fine-tuning
  - Vector Embeddings & Semantic Search Systems
  - Production API Development (FastAPI, Microservices)
  - MLOps & Model Deployment Pipelines
  - **Real-client project**: Deploy feature for Canadian startup sandbox environment

- **Assessment Protocol**:
  - 4 advanced technical examinations
  - Production system architecture review
  - Live coding interview simulation (Canadian startup standard)

- **Outcome**: Kactii Level-2 Certification & Interview Eligibility

### **Stage 3: International Placement**

- **Interview Pipeline**: Direct matching with Canadian startups seeking GenAI engineering interns
- **Compensation**: **CAD $15-20/hour** for internship roles (converted: ₹900-1,200/hour)
- **Conversion Path**: 85% of Level-2 certified interns receive full-time offers within 14 months

## PARTNERSHIP FRAMEWORK

### **College Obligations**
- Nominate 50-100 first-year CSE/IT/ECE students annually based on aptitude screening
- Provide 1-2 hours/week of schedule flexibility for live training sessions
- Designate a single faculty coordinator for administrative liaison

### **Kactii Academy Deliverables**
- End-to-end training delivery, assessment, and certification
- Direct startup matching and interview facilitation
- All payroll, compliance, and legal infrastructure for international employment
- Quarterly performance reporting to college administration
- ₹0 cost to college; student training fee subsidized through startup subscription model

### **Selection Criteria for Students**
- JEE Main percentile >75th OR state CET rank <5,000
- Proficiency in basic programming (Python/C++/Java)
- English communication assessment (B2 level or higher)
- Personal interview with Kactii admissions panel
