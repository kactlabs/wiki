## Research Paper - Contract

## Payment Structure

**On-Time Completion**: Full payment (100%) for projects delivered within the agreed deadline.

**Late Delivery Penalty**: Payment reduces by 10% for every 15-day period beyond the deadline.

## Certification & Recognition

**Research Certificate**: Participants who complete a minimum of 100 research hours are eligible for certification.

**Performance Stipend**: Top-performing researchers will receive additional stipend based on quality and contribution.
