# Pathway Influencers (PI)

## What Are Pathway Influencers?

Pathway Influencers (PI) are experts who provide clear, actionable paths to help you grow. Unlike general influencers, PIs offer structured guidance to become like them—making your journey intentional and achievable.

Kactii Academy uses this framework to identify and nurture future learners, helping them follow proven paths to success.
