## Employee Perks

Updated date: 2026-04-30


### F12 - Flexible 12
- You can work any 12 hours for the day

### Netflix Day
- You will be given a random date
- You can spend 12 hours on Binge-Watching Netflix
- Only English - This will help you to improve your communication skill
- No stupid TV shows
- Whoever contributed **260+ hours** in a month is eligible
- You need to document what you watched, so we will give more feedback on your communication improvements

### Bi-weekly/Monthly Random Place Coding
- You can go any random place near by your location and start coding for minimum 4 hrs (max: 12 hrs)
- We will fund a bit (Allowance: 200-600)
- You need to take a photo with proof

### Teach Public School
- Pick any public school and teach students for 2 hrs
- You get 500 INR for this activity
- We need a proof for it

### Monthly Team/Virtual Team Lunch and Learn
- Learn only Non-Tech things
  - Movies
  - Songs
  - Books
  - Travel
- Each gets minimum of 300 INR
- Whoever contributed **220+ hours** in a month is eligible

### Quarterly Flight Experience
- Top performer will be picked and given one way (minimum 1 hr, max 3 hrs) flight experience

### MacWeek
- We will rotate our official MacBook (used) between employees
- You will get MacBook experience
- Whoever contributed **50+ hours** in a week is eligible
