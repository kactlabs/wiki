## TeachTN



**TeachTN (Teach Tamil Nadu)** is a community-driven movement that empowers anyone to share knowledge of Generative AI with people across Tamil Nadu. Whether you are a student, professional, or tech enthusiast, you can join TeachTN to teach and learn the skills of the future. 

Our mission is to make GenAI accessible to every household in Tamil Nadu, helping individuals discover new opportunities, innovate, and thrive in the digital era. Together, we can build a smarter, future-ready Tamil Nadu.
