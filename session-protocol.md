## Session Protocol

A structured framework to ensure every learning session is engaging, interactive, and accountability-driven.

---

### Pre-Session

**Quiz (Mandatory)**

- A quiz must be conducted at the beginning of every session.
- The quiz covers topics from the previous session to reinforce retention and ensure continuous learning.
- All participants are expected to attempt the quiz — no exceptions.
- This serves as a warm-up and helps identify gaps before moving forward.

---

### Post-Session

**1. Handwritten Notes Submission (Mandatory)**

- Every participant must submit a photo of their handwritten notes in the designated channel after each session.
- Handwritten notes improve retention and demonstrate active engagement during the session.
- Notes should capture key concepts, diagrams, and personal takeaways.

**2. Favorite Subtopic Sharing (Mandatory)**

- After every session, each participant must share their favorite subtopic from that particular session.
- Along with the subtopic, they must explain *why* it stood out to them — what made it interesting, useful, or thought-provoking.
- This encourages reflective thinking and peer-to-peer knowledge exchange.

**3. AMA — Ask Me Anything (Mandatory)**

- Every participant must ask at least one question about the session content.
- Questions can be clarifications, deeper explorations, or connections to real-world applications.
- This builds a culture of curiosity and ensures no one leaves with unresolved doubts.

---

### Why This Protocol?

| Element | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Pre-session Quiz | Reinforces prior learning, keeps everyone accountable |
| Handwritten Notes | Encourages active listening and deeper processing |
| Favorite Subtopic | Promotes reflection and diverse perspectives |
| AMA Question | Ensures engagement and clears doubts |

---

### Key Rules

- All three post-session activities are **non-negotiable** for every participant.
- Submissions should happen on the same day as the session.
- Consistency in following this protocol builds discipline and maximizes learning outcomes.
