# Kact Among Us

## Objective:

- Identify the imposters among your teammates through clever description, deduction, and voting — while imposters try to stay hidden!.

## Roles:

- 2 Imposters – Pretend to know the word and avoid getting caught.
- Crewmates – Work together to identify the imposters.

## Word Assignment:

- Crewmates will be given a Common Word (e.g., “Coffee”).
- Imposters will be given the word "Imposter".
- Words should be assigned secretly by the moderator.

## Gameplay Structure:
### Round Format (3 Rounds Total):

- Each player gives one short clue/description about their word per round, without directly stating or obviously revealing the word.
- Everyone listens carefully to see who sounds "off" or inconsistent.
- Example for word "Coffee":
- Clue 1: "Many people need this to start their day."
- Clue 2: "Often comes with milk or sugar."
- Avoid saying the word directly or using rhyming hints like "starts with C".

## Voting Rules:
### Optional Voting (During the Round):

- If a player is suspicious during the round, anyone can call a vote to eliminate them.
- Team discusses briefly, then votes anonymously or openly.
- Majority vote = Player eliminated.

### Mandatory Voting (After Every Round):

- At the end of each round, there will be mandatory voting.
- All players must vote to eliminate one person.
- In case of a tie, the moderator may break the tie or skip the elimination for that round (optional rule).

## Winning Conditions:

### Crewmates Win if:

- Both imposters are identified and eliminated within 3 rounds.

### Imposters Win if:

- At least one imposter survives until the end of 3 rounds.
- OR too many crewmates are eliminated, and imposters are in equal numbers.

## Rules & Conduct:

- No revealing your word directly, even if you're eliminated.
- Maintain a fun and respectful environment — it's a game!
- Do not give clues that are overly vague or disruptive (e.g., "thing", "object", "I can't say").

## Tips for Crewmates:

- Listen for inconsistencies or generic clues.
- Watch body language and confidence in delivery.

## Tips for Imposters:

- Be vague, but not too vague.
- Echo ideas from crewmates to blend in.
- Give clues that could loosely fit the real word.
