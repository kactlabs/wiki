# Kactii AI — Investment Modes

We are raising funds for Kactii AI. Investors can choose from three investment modes based on their risk appetite and goals.

---

## Mode 1 — Startup Mode (High Growth)

**Minimum investment:** ₹2,00,000 (2L)

**Expected returns:** 5× to 20×+ on investment

**Time horizon:** 5 years

**Overview:**
This is the high-risk, high-reward path. You invest in Kactii AI as a startup believer. Your returns are directly tied to the company's growth and valuation milestones. Ideal for investors who understand the startup ecosystem and are comfortable with equity-style risk in exchange for outsized upside.

**Key details:**
- Returns are not guaranteed — they depend on company performance
- Suitable for angel investors and early believers
- Potential for 5× to 20×+ returns within a 5-year window

---

## Mode 2 — Fixed Returns (Stable Income)

**Minimum investment:** ₹2,00,000 (2L)

**Expected returns:** 13% per year

**Time horizon:** Flexible (annual payouts)

**Overview:**
This is the low-risk, steady-income path. You lend capital to Kactii AI and receive a guaranteed 13% annual return. Your principal is fully protected. Ideal for investors who want predictable returns without exposure to startup volatility.

**Key details:**
- 13% yearly returns, paid annually
- Full capital (principal) is guaranteed to be returned
- Suitable for conservative investors seeking stable passive income

---

## Mode 3 — Strategic Partner (Equity Ownership)

**Minimum investment:** ₹5,00,000 (5L)

**Expected returns:** Equity stake of 0.5% to 2% per ₹5L invested, plus dividends when profitable

**Time horizon:** Long-term (3–7 years)

**Overview:**
This is the ownership path. You become a direct equity holder in Kactii AI. Beyond financial returns, strategic partners gain co-branding opportunities, board observer rights, and a seat at the table as the company scales. Ideal for aligned investors who want to grow alongside Kactii AI as true stakeholders.

**Key details:**
- 0.5%–2% equity stake per ₹5L invested (based on valuation at time of investment)
- Board observer rights included
- Co-branding and strategic partnership opportunities
- Eligible for profit dividends once the company is profitable
- Suitable for institutional investors, HNIs, and mission-aligned partners

---

> **Note:** All investment modes are subject to a formal agreement and due diligence process. Returns for Mode 1 are projections based on growth targets and are not guaranteed. Mode 2 guarantee terms are governed by the signed investment agreement.
