# MiniAngels Club

**Tagline:** A New Way for Small Investors to Back Big Ideas from India

---

## 💡 The Vision

**MiniAngels** is a global community of independent investors who contribute a minimum of ₹5 lakhs (≈ $8,000 CAD) per year into a shared network that identifies, mentors, and invests in **early-stage Indian startups**.

### Our Mission
- 👉 Make *angel investing accessible* for smaller investors
- 👉 *Democratize cross-border startup funding*
- 👉 *Fuel India's next wave of innovation* with Canadian and global participation

---

## 🌍 The Core Idea

Every year, thousands of early-stage startups in India struggle to raise small but meaningful capital — often less than ₹25–50 lakhs. On the other side of the world, independent professionals and small investors in Canada and the U.S. want to participate in the India growth story but don't know where to start.

Traditional venture capital and angel groups often require large minimums (₹25L–₹1Cr) and insider access. **MiniAngels changes that.**

We're creating a **micro-angel network** that:
- Connects small accredited investors from **Canada, the U.S., and India**
- Curates **verified, early-stage Indian startups** ready for pre-seed/seed investment
- Allows members to **co-invest or pool capital** transparently
- Returns **profits or equity stakes** based on startup outcomes

This isn't a crowdfunding platform. It's a *member-driven investment club* designed for credibility, compliance, and community.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

### 1. Canadian Foundation
MiniAngels is being registered as a **Canadian investment club** — a private association of fewer than 50 members.

Each member commits to investing **a minimum of ₹5 lakhs (or ~$8,000 CAD)** annually.

The club is not a public offering or fund — it's a **closed, member-led syndicate**, compliant with Canadian securities exemptions.

### 2. Deal Discovery
Our team scouts startups across India through incubators, pitch events, and partner accelerators.

**Focus Areas:**
- Fintech, AI/ML, Sustainability, HealthTech, and Education sectors
- Early-stage founders seeking ₹25L–₹1Cr
- Clear problem-solving innovation with scalable potential

### 3. Due Diligence & Curation
Members get access to due diligence briefs, founder interviews, and valuation analysis before any investment decision.

Each deal goes through a **collective voting system** — every member has a say.

### 4. Cross-Border Investment Path
Investments will flow through a **Canadian entity (MiniAngels Inc.)** into an **Indian SPV (MiniAngels India Pvt Ltd)** that handles local deployment.

**This ensures:**
- RBI/FEMA compliance
- Clean taxation
- Transparent flow of returns

### 5. Returns
Members receive their share of profits or equity in proportion to their contribution in each startup.

MiniAngels earns a **small success or management fee (2–5%)** to sustain operations.

| Area | Compliance Route |
|------|------------------|
| **Registration** | Canadian corporation (Ontario or BC) |
| **Investor Type** | Accredited / eligible private members only |
| **Funds Flow** | Canadian account → Indian SPV (MiniAngels India Pvt Ltd) |

### Sample Investment Calculation: 15% Compound Interest

**Scenario:** Investment of $10,000 CAD with 15% annual compound interest over 2 years

**Formula:**
```
A = P(1 + r)^t
```

Where:
- P = Principal amount = $10,000 CAD
- r = Annual interest rate = 0.15 (15%)
- t = Time period = 2 years

**Calculation:**
```
A = 10,000 × (1.15)²
A = 10,000 × 1.3225
A = $13,225 CAD
```

**Results:**
- **Amount after 2 years:** $13,225 CAD
- **Total interest earned:** $13,225 - $10,000 = $3,225 CAD
- **Effective return:** 32.25% over 2 years

*Note: This is a simplified example for illustration. Actual startup returns vary significantly and may take 3-7 years to realize. Early-stage investments carry high risk and potential for both higher returns and total loss.*
