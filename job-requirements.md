# Job Requirements

```
Key Responsibilities:

Lead, guide, and motivate a team of interns and students.

Conduct regular Python training sessions to students(basic to advanced concepts).

Assign and evaluate assignments, projects, and coding tasks.

Track and record attendance, session details, and progress in Google Sheets.

Follow up with students to ensure timely task submissions and learning consistency.

Provide technical support and mentorship throughout the internship period.

Coordinate with management to share team progress and improvement areas.



Requirements:

Strong knowledge of Python programming (basic to intermediate/advanced).

Excellent communication and leadership skills.

Experience in mentoring, teaching, or team coordination preferred.

Ability to organize and manage multiple interns efficiently.

Familiarity with Google Workspace tools (Docs, Sheets, Meet, Drive).

Good problem-solving and interpersonal skills.
```

```
Key Responsibilities 

Lead and monitor a team of interns or students working on Python projects.

Conduct interactive Python training sessions (from basics to advanced topics).

Plan weekly learning goals and track progress consistently.

Assign, review, and evaluate coding assignments and projects.

Conduct code reviews and provide constructive technical feedback.

Record attendance, performance, and session details in Google Sheets.

```
Key Responsibilities 

Provide one-on-one mentoring to students needing additional support.

Debug and optimize code alongside learners when required.

Maintain effective communication between team members and management.

Prepare or update lesson materials and project documentation.

Organize mini hackathons, coding challenges, or group activities.

Evaluate final projects and prepare performance or feedback reports.

Support onboarding of new interns and help them understand workflows.
