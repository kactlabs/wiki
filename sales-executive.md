## About Kactii AI

Kactii AI is a GenAI startup building products and training programs. We're looking for an experienced sales professional who can bring in clients and grow with us.

* Make structured sales calls: intro, discovery, pitch, follow-up, close

* Visit clients onsite when required
* Meet monthly sales targets
* Log every call and update the CRM daily
* Share regular pipeline and sales reports

* Minimum 3 years of experience in sales or business development
* Strong calling, communication and negotiation skills
* Willingness to travel when needed for onsite client visits
* Disciplined in working remotely with daily reporting
* Proven record of meeting or exceeding targets
* Comfort with tech, EdTech, SaaS or AI products
