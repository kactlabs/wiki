# Niche 786 / Markkinarako 786  
## Student / Mentee Power Scale

**786 = your sweet spot**  
Not the smartest. Not the weakest.  
The most economically and operationally optimal learners.

---

## Level 10 — Predators  
**They will steal your business**

- Extremely sharp, opportunistic, low loyalty  
- Extract frameworks, positioning, and strategy  
- High probability of cloning your product/service  
- Ask meta-questions about how you think and decide  
- **Action:**  
  - Do not teach directly  
  - If unavoidable, keep abstract and charge premium  

---

## Level 9 — Rockets  
**They will learn and leave quickly**

- Very fast learners, execution-first  
- Treat you as a temporary resource  
- Low emotional or ecosystem attachment  
- **Action:**  
  - Short-term programs only  
  - High-ticket, fast-delivery formats  

---

## Level 8 — Strong Independents  
**Second-best students you can retain**

- High competence with partial loyalty  
- Grow fast but still value guidance  
- Potential collaborators, not replacements  
- **Action:**  
  - Give responsibility, not core secrets  
  - Retain via growth paths  

---

## Level 7 — Core Gold  
**Best students you can retain**

- Optimal balance of intelligence, humility, loyalty  
- Implement well and seek feedback  
- Promote you and grow with you  
- **Action:**  
  - Invest heavily  
  - Build inner circle and long-term roadmap  

---

## Level 6 — Reliable Executors  
**Third-best students you can retain**

- Moderate intelligence, high consistency  
- Thrive on structure and repetition  
- Ideal for scaling operations  
- **Action:**  
  - SOPs, checklists, repeatable workflows  

---

## Level 5 — Average Mass  
**Steady but unremarkable learners**

- Learn slowly but consistently  
- Progress depends on environment  
- Require motivation and accountability  
- **Action:**  
  - Group programs  
  - Recorded content  

---

## Level 4 — Confused but Willing  
**Motivated but unfocused**

- Jump between ideas and trends  
- Lack clarity, not intelligence  
- **Action:**  
  - Narrow scope aggressively  
  - Create short wins  

---

## Level 3 — Dependent Learners  
**Need constant hand-holding**

- Forget concepts quickly  
- Repeated questions  
- Low abstraction ability  
- **Action:**  
  - Teach via recorded material only  
  - Avoid 1:1 time  

---

## Level 2 — Memory Leaks  
**Forget again and again**

- Poor retention systems  
- Teaching feels like starting over  
- **Action:**  
  - Do not teach personally  
  - Redirect to basics  

---

## Level 1 — Time Wasters  
**They will never learn**

- No execution or curiosity  
- Externalize blame  
- Drain time and energy  
- **Action:**  
  - Hard reject  
  - No engagement  

---

## The 786 Rule — Summary

- Avoid Levels **9–10** → they outgrow or replace you  
- Avoid Levels **1–3** → they drain you  
- Build on Levels **6–8** → they scale you  
- Protect Level **7** → they become your legacy
