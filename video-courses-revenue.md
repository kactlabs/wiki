## Kactii Academy - Video Course Revenue Map - 2 Years

This projection assumes the following:

* Price per course: **₹300**
* Number of courses: **10**
* Starting audience: **8 buyers per course per month**
* Monthly growth in buyers: **+2 buyers per course**
* Commission to House:

  * **Year 1:** 20%
  * **Year 2:** 40%
* One-time creation fee payout to each course maker: **₹2,000**
* Total creation payout in Year 1: **₹20,000 for 10 courses**

## Business Insight

The second year produces more than five times the net revenue of Year 1 due to:

* Audience compounding (2 new buyers per course per month).
* Commission doubling from 20% to 40% for the house after Year 1.
* Zero repeated acquisition cost for already-built courses.

This model becomes significantly more profitable with scale. Adding more courses or accelerating buyer growth creates exponential return without proportional increase in operating cost.
