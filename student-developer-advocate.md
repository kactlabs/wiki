# 📢 Join Featurepreneur as a Student Developer Advocate!

**Are you passionate about tech, community, and helping other students grow?**  
Featurepreneur is looking for motivated, self-driven students to become **Student Developer Advocates** in our fast-growing tech community!

---

## 🎯 **What is a Student Developer Advocate?**

A Student Developer Advocate acts as a **bridge** between Featurepreneur and your campus tech community.  
You’ll help students learn, build, and grow by organizing events, sharing knowledge, and creating a collaborative environment.

---

## 💡 **What You’ll Do**

✅ Organize Study Jams, workshops, and hackathons.  
✅ Create engaging content—tutorials, demos, or blog posts.  
✅ Grow and manage a vibrant student tech community.  
✅ Collect feedback and share it with our team to improve resources.  
✅ Represent Featurepreneur at your campus and beyond!

---

## 🎁 **What You’ll Get**

✨ Mentorship from industry professionals.  
✨ Exclusive access to resources and training.  
✨ Guided training in the **GenAI tech stack**—learn cutting-edge AI tools and frameworks.  
✨ Opportunity to **work on real GenAI projects** with guidance from our mentors.  
✨ Recognition for your contributions (certificates, swag, goodies!).  
✨ Boost your resume and career prospects with real-world developer advocacy experience.
✨ **Hands-on experience in publishing Python libraries on PyPI.**  
   - 📌 Example: [How we contribute to PyPI](https://wiki.kactii.com/pypi-python-library-contribution.html)  


---

## ✅ **Who Can Apply?**

- University/college students passionate about technology.  
- Active in campus tech communities or clubs.  
- Good communication & teamwork skills.  
- Eager to share, teach, and lead!

---

## 📅 **How to Apply**

Interested? Send your profile/resume to **info+fpr@kactii.com**.
