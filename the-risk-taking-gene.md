## The Risk Taking Gene

**Note:** Why some people take too big huge take and what's the science behind it?

Some people will get bored with a 9–5 life or not be interested in such a lifestyle, though the statistics show that a 9–5 life is far safer than the Entrepreneurship journey!

Someone said Startup is a gamble where you have to say “All In”! Though the All-in strategy looks great in the media but it puts a lot of pressure on the risk-takers.

Some failed entrepreneurs committed suicide, divorced their partners, and shrunk their lives for this entrepreneurial journey. You might wonder what is fueling these entrepreneurs to take such a great risk, even though the success rate is less than 2%.

In my upcoming Book, “The Risk-taking Gene”, I will talk about why those entrepreneurs take such a huge risk.

I am talking to many entrepreneurs from various industries, including startups to big companies, to understand their reasons behind those actions.
