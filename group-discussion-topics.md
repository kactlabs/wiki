## Group Discussion Topics

1. Explain human evolution from its earliest beginnings, highlighting both the mistakes humanity made and the innovations that shaped progress. The explanation should trace the journey from primitive survival to advanced civilization, explicitly covering key milestones such as the conceptualization of the circle (Archimedes), the invention of radio, the discovery and application of electricity, the rise of the internet, and the emergence of Generative AI.

2. What do you think about Casteism? 
Whether it's good or for society? 
If Good, how good it is? Is it 1 out 10 good or 10 out of 10 good. 
If Bad, how good it is? Is it 1 out 10 bad or 10 out of 10 bad.
